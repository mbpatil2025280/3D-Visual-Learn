Understanding Oscillation

Oscillation is a repetitive back-and-forth motion around a central point, known as the equilibrium position.

  • Amplitude: The maximum distance from the center.
  • Frequency: How many cycles occur per second.
  • Restoring Force: The force that pulls the object back to the center.

In this 3D model, the glowing sphere follows Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM). Notice how it slows down at the peaks and moves fastest through the center.
