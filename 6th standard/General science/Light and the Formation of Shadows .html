💡 Light and the Formation of Shadows

What is Light?

Light is a form of energy that makes things visible. It travels in straight lines and at incredible speed — about 300,000 km per second!

💡 Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to travel from the Sun to the Earth.

Sources of Light

  • Natural sources: Sun, stars, fire, fireflies.
  • Artificial sources: Electric bulbs, torches, candles.

Transparent, Translucent, Opaque

  • Transparent: Allows all light through – glass, water.
  • Translucent: Allows some light through – frosted glass, oily paper.
  • Opaque: Blocks all light – wood, stone, metal.

How Shadows Form

A shadow forms when an opaque object blocks light. Shadows have two parts:

  • Umbra: Darkest part of the shadow (no light reaches).
  • Penumbra: Lighter outer region (partial light reaches).
