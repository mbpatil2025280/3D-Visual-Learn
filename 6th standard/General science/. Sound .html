🔊 Sound

What is Sound?

Sound is a form of energy produced by vibrating objects. It travels through a medium (solid, liquid, or gas) in the form of waves. We hear sound when these waves reach our ears.

💡 Sound cannot travel through vacuum — that's why space is completely silent!

How is Sound Produced?

  • Sound is produced by the vibration of objects.
  • A vibrating object creates compressions and rarefactions in the surrounding medium.
  • Examples: guitar strings, vocal cords, a ringing bell.

Properties of Sound

  • Pitch: How high or low a sound is (depends on frequency).
  • Loudness: How loud or soft a sound is (depends on amplitude).
  • Speed: Sound travels at ~340 m/s in air at room temperature.

Key Terms

  • Vibration: Back and forth motion of an object.
  • Frequency: Number of vibrations per second (Hertz, Hz).
  • Amplitude: Size of vibration — determines loudness.
  • Echo: Reflected sound heard after original sound.
