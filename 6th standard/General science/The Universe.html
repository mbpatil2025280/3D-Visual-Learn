🌌 The Universe

What is the Universe?

The universe is everything that exists — all matter, energy, space, and time. It is unimaginably vast and contains billions of galaxies, each with billions of stars.

💡 The observable universe is about 93 billion light-years in diameter — and it's still expanding!

Our Solar System

  • The Solar System consists of the Sun and all objects orbiting it.
  • 8 Planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
  • Also includes: dwarf planets (Pluto), asteroids, comets, and moons.
  • Earth is the only known planet with life.

Stars and Galaxies

  • Star: A giant ball of hot, glowing gas that produces light and heat by nuclear fusion. The Sun is our nearest star.
  • Galaxy: A massive system of billions of stars, gas, and dust. Our galaxy is the Milky Way.
  • Constellation: A group of stars forming a pattern, e.g., Orion, Big Dipper.

The Moon

  • The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite.
  • It completes one orbit around Earth in about 27.3 days.
  • Moon phases: New Moon → Crescent → Quarter → Gibbous → Full Moon.
  • Causes tides on Earth due to gravitational pull.
