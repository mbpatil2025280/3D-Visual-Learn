🥗 Nutrition and Diet

What is Nutrition?

Nutrition is the process by which living organisms take in and use food for energy, growth, and repair. A good diet contains all the nutrients our body needs in the right amounts.

Nutrients and Their Functions

  • Carbohydrates: Main source of energy. Found in rice, wheat, bread.
  • Proteins: Build and repair body tissues. Found in meat, eggs, pulses.
  • Fats: Store energy, protect organs. Found in butter, oils, nuts.
  • Vitamins: Protect from disease. Found in fruits and vegetables.
  • Minerals: Strengthen bones and teeth. Found in milk, green leafy veg.
  • Water: Essential for all body functions.
  • Roughage (Fibre): Aids digestion. Found in whole grains, vegetables.
💡 A balanced diet contains all 7 nutrients in the right proportion for a healthy body!

Deficiency Diseases

  • Scurvy: Lack of Vitamin C – bleeding gums.
  • Rickets: Lack of Vitamin D – weak bones.
  • Anaemia: Lack of Iron – tiredness, pale skin.
  • Goitre: Lack of Iodine – swollen neck.
