🔄 Motion and Types of Motion

What is Motion?

An object is said to be in motion when it changes its position with time. Motion requires a reference point — a fixed point from which we measure the change in position.

💡 Everything in the universe is in motion — even the atoms inside a perfectly still rock!

Types of Motion

  • Linear Motion: Movement in a straight line. Example: a car on a straight road.
  • Circular Motion: Movement in a circular path. Example: a fan blade, Earth around the Sun.
  • Oscillatory Motion: Back and forth movement. Example: a pendulum, a swing.
  • Random Motion: Irregular, unpredictable movement. Example: a butterfly flying.

Speed and Distance

  • Distance: How far an object has moved.
  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time
  • Example: A car covers 100 km in 2 hours → Speed = 50 km/h.
