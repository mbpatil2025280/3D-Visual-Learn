🌱 The Living World

What is Living?

Living things are organisms that are alive. They grow, respire, reproduce, respond to stimuli, and carry out various life processes. Plants, animals, fungi, and bacteria are all living things.

💡 There are estimated to be over 8.7 million species of living things on Earth!

Characteristics of Living Things

  • Growth: Living things grow and develop over time.
  • Respiration: They use oxygen to release energy from food.
  • Nutrition: They need food for energy and growth.
  • Reproduction: They produce offspring.
  • Response: They react to changes in the environment.
  • Excretion: They remove waste from their body.

Classification

  • Kingdom Plantae: Plants – make their own food through photosynthesis.
  • Kingdom Animalia: Animals – cannot make their own food.
  • Kingdom Fungi: Mushrooms, moulds – feed on dead matter.
  • Kingdom Monera: Bacteria – single-celled without nucleus.
