🌿 Diversity in Living Things & Their Classification

Why Classify?

There are millions of living things on Earth. Scientists organise them into groups to make them easier to study and understand. This is called classification.

💡 Carolus Linnaeus is known as the "Father of Taxonomy" — he created the modern system of naming organisms.

The Five Kingdoms

  • Monera: Bacteria – single-celled, no cell nucleus.
  • Protista: Amoeba, algae – mostly single-celled with nucleus.
  • Fungi: Mushrooms, moulds – absorb nutrients from surroundings.
  • Plantae: All plants – make food by photosynthesis.
  • Animalia: All animals – consume food for energy.

Plant Classification

  • Non-flowering plants: Ferns, mosses, pine trees.
  • Flowering plants: Rose, mango, wheat.

Animal Classification

  • Vertebrates: Have a backbone – fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals.
  • Invertebrates: No backbone – insects, worms, jellyfish, spiders.
