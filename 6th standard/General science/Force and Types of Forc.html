💪 Force and Types of Force

What is Force?

A force is a push or pull acting on an object. Force can change the shape, speed, or direction of an object. It is measured in Newtons (N).

💡 You cannot see force, but you can see its effects — like a ball rolling when you kick it!

Types of Force

  • Contact Forces: Act only when objects are touching.
    • Muscular Force – force by muscles (pushing a door).
    • Frictional Force – opposes motion between surfaces.
    • Spring Force – force stored in a stretched/compressed spring.
  • Non-Contact Forces: Act without touching.
    • Gravitational Force – attraction between masses (gravity).
    • Magnetic Force – attraction/repulsion of magnets.
    • Electrostatic Force – force between charged objects.

Effects of Force

  • Makes a stationary object move.
  • Stops a moving object.
  • Changes the speed or direction of a moving object.
  • Changes the shape or size of an object.
