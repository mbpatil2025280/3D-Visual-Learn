🧴 Substances in Daily Use

What are Substances?

A substance is any kind of matter with specific properties. Every day we use dozens of substances — from soap to salt, from plastic to metal.

Common Daily Substances

  • Water (H₂O): Universal solvent, used for drinking, cooking, cleaning.
  • Salt (NaCl): Used in cooking; a natural preservative.
  • Soap: Made from fats and alkali; removes dirt by dissolving in water.
  • Vinegar: Acidic substance; used in cooking and food preservation.
  • Baking Soda: Alkali; used in cooking and cleaning.
  • Plastics: Synthetic substances used in containers, bags, bottles.
  • Metals: Copper, iron, aluminium used in utensils and tools.
💡 Soap molecules have one end that loves water and another end that loves grease — that's how they clean!

Harmful vs Useful

  • Some substances like bleach and acids are useful but can be harmful if misused.
  • Always read labels on household chemicals.
  • Avoid plastic where possible — it takes hundreds of years to decompose.
