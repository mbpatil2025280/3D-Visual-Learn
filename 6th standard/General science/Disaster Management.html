⛑️ Disaster Management

What is a Disaster?

A disaster is a sudden event that causes great damage, destruction, and suffering. Disasters can be natural or man-made.

💡 India is one of the most disaster-prone countries in the world due to its geographical location.

Types of Disasters

  • Natural Disasters: Earthquakes, floods, cyclones, droughts, tsunamis, landslides.
  • Man-Made Disasters: Industrial accidents, fires, oil spills, nuclear accidents.

Disaster Management

  • Prevention: Take steps to avoid disasters (e.g., building earthquake-resistant structures).
  • Preparedness: Be ready to respond (emergency kits, evacuation drills).
  • Response: Act immediately after a disaster (rescue operations, first aid).
  • Recovery: Restore normalcy (rebuilding, rehabilitation).

Safety Tips

  • Keep an emergency kit with water, food, and medicines.
  • Know your nearest evacuation route.
  • Learn basic first aid.
  • Stay calm and follow instructions from authorities.
