🌍 Natural Resources – Air, Water and Land

What are Natural Resources?

Natural resources are materials found in nature that are useful to humans. The three most important natural resources are air, water, and land.

Air

  • Air is a mixture of gases: ~78% Nitrogen, ~21% Oxygen, and ~1% other gases.
  • Oxygen is essential for breathing and burning.
  • Carbon dioxide is used by plants during photosynthesis.
  • Air Pollution: Caused by vehicle emissions, factories, burning of fuels.
💡 Trees are called the "lungs of the Earth" because they absorb CO₂ and release oxygen!

Water

  • About 71% of Earth's surface is covered with water, but only 3% is fresh water.
  • Water cycle: evaporation → condensation → precipitation → collection.
  • Water Pollution: Caused by industrial waste, sewage, agricultural chemicals.

Land (Soil)

  • Land provides space for living beings and growing food.
  • Soil contains minerals, water, air, and microorganisms.
  • Land Pollution: Caused by plastic waste, pesticides, deforestation.
  • Conservation: Afforestation, crop rotation, reduce plastic use.
